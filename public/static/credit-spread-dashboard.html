    How it works

    Sell a near option for premium, buy a farther one as insurance. Your max loss is fixed. Profit comes from time decay when price stays away from your sold strike.

    Low-capital rules

    • Risk 1-2% of capital per trade. If 1 lot exceeds it, narrow the width or skip.
    • Sell about 10-15 delta. Prefer 2-8 days to expiry.
    • Credit should be at least 25-30% of width.
    • Exit at 50% of credit as profit, or at stop (1.5x credit loss).
    • Higher VIX means richer premium; low VIX means thin credit, so skip.
    • Skip event days (RBI, budget, results, global shocks).
    • Be careful on expiry day; gamma risk is highest.
    • Stop for the day after 2 straight losses or 3% daily loss.
    • Never average a losing spread or remove the hedge leg.

    Entry steps

    • 1. Mark support/resistance and highest PE/CE OI strikes.
    • 2. Sell strike beyond that OI wall, not inside range.
    • 3. Buy hedge leg first, then sell leg.
    • 4. Place stop alert on the spread or on Nifty level.
    • 5. Exit both legs together.

    Reality check

    High win rate does not mean safe: one max-loss trade can erase several winners. Realistic goal is steady small gains, not quick big profit. Paper trade for a few weeks first. Margin, brokerage, taxes and lot size change, so verify with your broker.

    3. Iron condor: hedge the other side

    Add the opposite spread to your trade (Bull Put + Bear Call, or the reverse). Best on range-bound normal days.
    Only one side can lose at expiry, so max loss uses the wider side. Broker margin may be lower than two spreads; verify.

    4. Hedging rules

    • Your long leg is already the hedge. Extra hedges cost premium or margin.
    • Normal day: sell far OTM, set a stop, no extra hedge needed.
    • Range-bound market: add the other side (iron condor) for more credit.
    • Spot within 0.5 SD of sold strike: exit both legs, do not add a naked leg.
    • Event or gap risk: skip, or cut lots in half.
    • 50% profit: book it, or move stop to break-even.
    • Never remove the long leg, never leg out leaving a short leg naked, never average a loser.

      6. Intraday rules

      • Trade with the trend. No bull put on a falling day.
      • Sell 10-15 delta beyond an OI wall and support/resistance.
      • Take profit at 40-50% of credit. Do not wait for full premium.
      • Exit at the stop price or the index exit level, whichever comes first.
      • Time stop: if flat after 1-2 hours, close.
      • Max 1-2 trades. Stop after 2 losses in a row.
      • Exit both legs together. Never average or widen the stop.
