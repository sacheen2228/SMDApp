# Market Structure and Stop-Loss Geography

## Basics
- Uptrend: HH + HL. Downtrend: LH + LL.
- **BOS (break of structure):** break of last swing in trend direction = continuation.
- **CHoCH (change of character):** uptrend breaks last HL (or downtrend breaks last LH) = first reversal warning.
- Market states: trending (trade breakouts and pullbacks), range-bound (edges only or stay out), choppy/inside previous day range (reduce size or skip).

## Timeframes
| TF | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Daily / 1-hr | Trend and major levels |
| 15-min | Setup and structure |
| 5-min | Precise entry |
Trade with the higher-timeframe trend. Counter-trend needs stronger confirmation and smaller size.

## Where stops cluster
| Who | Stops sit |
|---|---|
| Long buyers | Below swing lows, previous day low, supports |
| Short sellers | Above swing highs, previous day high, resistances |
| Call writers | Above sold strike, or when premium doubles/triples |
| Put writers | Below sold strike, or when premium doubles/triples |
| Breakout traders | Back inside the range |

Price often **sweeps** these zones: pokes through, triggers stops, reverses (fake breakout / stop hunt).

## Using this
- Do not put your stop exactly on an obvious level; add a buffer or use a candle close beyond it (and size down since the loss can be larger).
- **Sweep and reclaim:** price dips below support then closes back above quickly -> call setup, stop below the sweep low. Mirror for puts at resistance.
- Failed breakout/trap reversal is often the fastest-moving setup because trapped traders exit.

## Time windows
- Avoid first 10-15 minutes; let the opening range form.
- Better windows: ~9:30-11:00 and ~1:45-3:00.
- ~11:30-1:30 is often sideways; theta hurts.
